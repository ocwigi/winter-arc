Winter Arc habit tracker

Open index.html in a browser to try the MVP.

What it tracks:
- Gym: 4 times per week
- Sauna: 3 times per week
- Protein: 165g per day
- Calories: 1800 or less per day
- Supplements: daily
- Steps: 10,000 per day
- No alcohol: daily
- Weigh in: daily, green once weight or body fat is logged
- Weight goal: 81kg starting point to 75kg target
- Body fat goal: 22% starting point to 15% target
- AI learning: 60 minutes per day
- Winter win: a flexible bonus habit for anything helpful

How it works:
- Red means not done yet.
- Green means done.
- Numeric habits have quick buttons and a box for typing your own number.
- The season runs from June 1, 2026 to August 31, 2026, which is 92 days.
- Use Previous, Next, or the date picker to go back and update older days.
- The app opens on the current day, so after midnight it naturally starts a fresh day.
- Steps now have volume targets: 70,000 per week and 300,000 per month.
- AI learning also has weekly and monthly volume targets.
- Body goals show current weight/body fat, percentage complete, and how much remains.
- Body fat progress is measured in percentage points, such as 22% to 15% being a 7-point drop.
- On-track scores compare your current hit rate against the pace needed for the winter goal.
- Body trend draws a simple chart for logged weight and body fat readings.
- Winter quests turn key goals into visuals, including a mountain climb for weekly steps.
- The Habit scorecard shows every habit across the full 92-day winter period with green, red, and grey dots.
- Weekly habits such as gym and sauna only turn red when a completed week misses the weekly target. Non-training days stay optional grey.
- Tap a dot or a day tile to jump back to that date and update it.
- Your history is saved in the browser on the device you use.
- The Export button copies or downloads your saved history.
